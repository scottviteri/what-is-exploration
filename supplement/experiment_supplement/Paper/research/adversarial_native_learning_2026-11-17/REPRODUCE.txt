Reproducing the finite learned-target pilot

Run commands from Paper/research/adversarial_native_learning_2026-11-17/.
The programs require Python, NumPy, SciPy, Torch and Matplotlib. Exact installed
versions are recorded in main/manifest.json; training uses CPU float64 and one
Torch thread. No downloaded model, GPU, service or credentials are needed.

1. Read EXPLAINER.pdf and DEVELOPMENT.txt for the objective, access assumptions,
   controls and separation of development from registered main outcomes.

2. Run the independent semantic checks:
   OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 test_pilot.py

3. Reproduce all main runs into a NEW output directory:
   OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 run_pilot.py \
       --config main_config.json --output reproduced-main

   The runner refuses to overwrite an existing manifest. It snapshots and
   hashes the sources before execution and refuses a mixture of source versions.
   main/source/ preserves the code used for the original run. main/manifest.json
   also contains the complete configuration and dependency versions.

4. Replay the saved data, policies, decoder scores and LP profiles independently:
   OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 test_pilot.py \
       --results reproduced-main --output reproduced-main-independent-replay.json

5. Generate figures and machine-readable summaries:
   python3 report_results.py --input reproduced-main --output reproduced-report

6. Rebuild the authored explainer and results explanation:
   latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error EXPLAINER.tex
   latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error RESULTS.tex

Each data.json stores sampled-response counts and hashes of the original episode
bytes. The seed and sampler reconstruct those episodes; no training data are
silently omitted. training.json stores all collector/decoder/generator states
needed for checkpoint evaluation, including candidate decoders at proposal time.
audits.json stores separate true-law and empirical-law LP profiles and witnesses.

Adversarial, random and hardest_uniform have matched candidate-fitting and
collector-update budgets. Generator updates have their own counts and overhead.
all_targets is a diagnostic with extra decoder work, not an equal-cost baseline.
The world labels available to the trainer are absent from every collector and
decoder input. Only each frozen collector's two-step record is evaluated.

The main study is empirical evidence on these three fixed world classes.
Numerical LP residuals and dual checks are not interval proofs or Lean proofs.
No convergence certificate follows from search stagnation, and this pilot makes
no numerical-approximation claim for the eventual reference objective J_w.

Raw per-run payloads and the detailed SUMMARY.json are kept locally but excluded
from Git. The smaller COMPACT_SUMMARY.json retains all group metrics and signed
paired differences. Fresh clones need step 3 before replaying saved runs.
