# Longer-budget objective selection against fixed short references

Authorized by the lead author on 24 September 2026 after discussing the difference between sampling policy candidates and optimizing a finite objective. This is the budget-specific option from the parent design. Each collector is optimized afresh for its total interaction budget; collectors at different budgets need not share prefixes. No continuation or monotone single-policy error curve is claimed.

## Question and fixed quantities

How well do objective-selected collectors at three, four and five interactions reproduce a fixed collection of three-interaction records? Each case retains exactly its archived candidate transition/observation models, binary interface, original initial state, and uniform prior. The actual model is unknown and does not change within a run. Collectors are full-history adaptive randomized policies. The decoder uses the source record and private randomness; it knows the candidate class but not the actual model and cannot take additional observations or reset the environment.

Use all 22 existing classes. Before any new optimization, freeze the lexically first already verified cell for each of the six methods in each class: whole-world information gain, whole-world posterior Brier gain, the specified categorical pseudo-count, uniform, equal-weight native128, and native minimax. Copy model and policy arrays, bind hashes and physically replay every reference. The same six records are targets for every method and budget in that case. These are existing selected numerical optima/controls, not a sample of all optima. Reference choice is independent of the new audit. Prior study failures and unfavorable cases remain untouched.

For each selected collector E at total budget t, evaluate delta(E,F_reference) in both source/reference method directions where available. Hold the target at three interactions. Report numerical lower/upper bounds, and classify at absolute errors 0.05, 0.02 and 0.01 with a 1e-9 comparison margin. A crossing requires an upper bound below the tolerance; non-reproduction requires a lower bound above it; overlapping intervals are unresolved. No crossing at one budget implies a crossing at subsequent budgets because the independently optimized collectors may differ. These are acquisition-budget comparisons, not stopping times, universal rankings or eventual sufficiency tests.

## Objectives and selection

Information is expected entropy reduction in the full model label, with base-two logarithms. Brier is expected gain in squared norm of the full model posterior. Both use terminal time t and the original full-support prior. Neither is a selected feature reward or raw-observation prediction loss. Categorical pseudo-count accumulates 1/sqrt(1+N_o) over raw observed labels with counts initially zero; retain the exact declared categorical specification and do not generalize its findings to every density-model pseudo-count implementation. Uniform independently samples either action with probability one half.

Native weighted minimizes the uniform average of deficiencies to all 128 deterministic depth-three trees. Native minimax minimizes their maximum. The full 128-target library and weights stay fixed at every source budget. Every randomized three-step reference is a mixture of these trees, so native minimax directly bounds its error. This is an intentional capability comparison within the covered family, not held-out-target transfer, infinite-rich eventual J_w, or a claim that the native scalar is uniquely correct. The earlier three-to-four study retains its separate role.

At t=3 use the exact frozen reference policies, preserving their existing selected-optimum qualification. At t=4,5 optimize from scratch. Posterior and count rewards use finite-tree dynamic programming with uniform mixing only over exactly tied float64 action values. An independent recurrence recomputes the optimum; bounded validation compares the separate realization LP. Native objectives use the validated dual-cut formulation with all syntactic source histories included in every cut, including histories absent from the incumbent. Its master lower bound and feasible decoder upper bound must close to 2e-7. Fixed algorithm/tie settings are not selected using reference outcomes. Failed or incomplete solvers may leave a feasible audited incumbent, clearly labeled incomplete and excluded from certified-optimum comparisons.

## Stages and resources

One numerical worker, pinned to CPU 10, one thread, 8 GiB address-space cap, eight-hour total wall budget, 4 GiB output cap and 15 GiB free-disk reserve. The host cgroup provides 10.2 CPU equivalents and about 28.9 GiB RAM; the much larger visible host totals are not the allocation. No GPU reservation. Shared-machine timings are descriptive.

First replay/audit all 132 frozen reference collectors. Then run four mechanism cases at t=4 and t=5: sensors_0.1_0.1, delayed_0.1_0.1, irreversible_0.1_0.1, and fresh_hmm_2026096800_q4_s3_c0.2. These are the first original entries in their families, with known old results, not fresh confirmation. Retain the constant-Brier irreversible case. Expand t=4 to the remaining 18 classes for a method with at least one accepted t=4 pilot cell; expand a method to pilot t=5 only after all its t=4 pilot cells are certified, and to remaining t=5 cases only after all its t=5 pilot cells are certified. These gates use computational feasibility and verification, never superiority over another method.

Native planning caps: 300 seconds at t=4, 600 seconds at t=5, 400 cutting-plane iterations. Reference decoder calls have 60 seconds each. Whole-worker caps are 1,500/2,400 seconds; the supervisor separately bounds arithmetic verification. A STOP file ends the supervisor and its own active child group only. Preserve unfinished output and report failures/skips/pending counts. No unregistered solver retry, changed tolerance, reduced native library or selected winner is substituted after failure.

## Verification and follow-up

Retain literal model arrays, full conditional policy rows, source kernels, target IDs/weights, native cuts and selected master certificates, every selected native training decoder, and all six reference decoder/decision witnesses. Independently replay physical dynamics and rewards, reconstruct native targets and cut slopes, bind saved LP matrices to source flow/objective/cuts, replay primal and box-repaired dual bounds, and check every original-source reference witness. Arithmetic is float64 with stated residual checks, not interval arithmetic or a new Lean theorem. Negative checks deliberately corrupt witnesses. Source snapshots and hashes refuse changed dependencies.

A registered subsequent diagnostic asks how much original information/Brier reward must be sacrificed to simulate a particular reference within 0.02. It is eligible on pilot cases/budgets only when the selected policy's checked lower error exceeds 0.02+1e-9. It will be a separate favorable, reference-aware compatibility calculation, never a replacement primary collector. Its numerical wrapper and validation must be completed before those jobs launch; the initial supervisor runs only the primary grid. Report diagnostic implementation/execution status separately. No all-optima or typical-learner inference follows from an existential compatibility result.

No manuscript, shared formal ledger, Git or Overleaf edit is part of this campaign. Source qualification for the currently compared rewards remains as stated; no archived ICM/RND/predictive score is silently reintroduced.
